Consenting tenant members can join cooperative challenges, contribute verified activity within participation intervals and per-member caps, and leave under an explicit retention policy. PostgreSQL transactions preserve one final settlement and completion intent across concurrent contributions and retries. Participant controls and the operator console share the challenge service and expose aggregate progress without private activity.

Generated app package-role metadata includes the cooperative challenge packages.
