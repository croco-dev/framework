Customers can save and resume private resources through current permission checks,
with revisioned completion, pinning, and removal that suppresses automatic recent
updates. PostgreSQL preserves scoped state and audited retention policies; React
saved items and the operator console use the same policy service.

The public Problem registry includes saved-intent input, access, administration,
and revision conflict codes with their existing category recovery policies.
