Referral funnels count every claim in the selected program and optional family, including conversions beyond the first 1,000 records. Stored qualification evidence keeps signup and qualification totals stable through uncertain or rejected payouts. Fulfillment totals reflect the current attribution state. Custom stores implement `countFunnelAttributions` to supply complete totals. Upgrade the referral service and store together.
